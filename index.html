About Me

I'm Hanif Nurrohman, an Instrumentation and Control Engineering graduate with a passion for designing systems that exist at the intersection of physical and digital worlds.

My core expertise spans the full embedded systems stack — from schematic capture and PCB layout to firmware development and cloud-connected data pipelines. I believe the best engineering solutions are both functionally sound and elegantly designed.

Whether it's reverse-engineering a power converter, building an IoT wristband, or deploying an automatic weather station to the cloud, I bring the same level of care and precision to every project.
